Photos for the dinner cards
===========================

Drop a photo in this folder named after the meal id and it automatically
replaces that meal's icon on the page. Nothing else to change.

  Example:  spag_bol.jpg      (or .jpeg .png .webp)

The meal ids are the "id" values in ../../meals.json, e.g.:
  spag_bol, carbonara, chicken_curry, homemade_pizza, beef_stroganoff,
  shepherds_pie, roast_beef, sausages_bbq, pumpkin_soup, laksa ...

Tip: a small landscape photo (~600px wide) keeps the page fast. The best
photos for recognition are your own family's dinners.
